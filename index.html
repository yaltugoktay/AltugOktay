Privacy Policy - Shop Pin Helper

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Shop Pin Helper is a private tool used only by Altug Oktay, an e-commerce brand manager and trainer based in Istanbul, Turkey, on his own computer. It is not offered to other users.

What it accesses

Only the owner's own Pinterest business account, through the official Pinterest API, with the owner's permission (OAuth). It reads the owner's boards and creates Pins of the owner's own product photos, one by one, after the owner reviews and approves each Pin.

What it stores

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. Pin and board data are used only to show the owner's own boards and to avoid duplicate Pins. No data from other Pinterest users is collected.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. No data is used for advertising.

Deletion

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in Pinterest settings; the stored tokens and data are then deleted from the computer.
